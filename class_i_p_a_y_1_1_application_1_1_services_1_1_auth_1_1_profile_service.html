async Task< ProfileResult< StartEmailChangeResultDto > > StartEmailChangeAsync (int userId, StartEmailChangeDto dto)
 Email change, step 1 (email only, no password): the new address is free. Changes nothing in our own data yet.
async Task< ProfileResult< AuthResponse > > ConfirmEmailChangeAsync (int userId, ConfirmEmailChangeDto dto)
